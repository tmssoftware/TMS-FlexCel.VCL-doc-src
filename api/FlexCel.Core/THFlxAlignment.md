# THFlxAlignment Enumeration

Horizontal Alignment on a cell\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|general|0|General Alignment\. \(Text to the left, numbers to the right and Errors and booleans centered\)|
|left|1|Aligned to the left\.<br />|
|center|2|Horizontally centered on the cell\.<br />|
|right|3|Aligned to the right\.<br />|
|fill|4|Repeat the text to fill the cell width\.<br />|
|justify|5|Justify with spaces the text so it fills the cell width\.<br />|
|center\_across\_selection|6|Centered on a group of cells\.<br />|
|distributed|7|Justified\.<br />|
