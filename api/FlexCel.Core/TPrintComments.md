# TPrintComments Enumeration

Determines how the comments will be displayed when printing\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|None|0|Comments won't be printed\.<br />|
|AtEndOfSheet|1|Comments will be printed at the end of the sheet\.<br />|
|AsDisplayed|2|Comments will be printed as they show in the page\. If they are closed, they won't print, but if they are open they will\.<br />|
