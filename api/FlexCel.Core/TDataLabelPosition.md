# TDataLabelPosition Enumeration

Defines where a label is displayed on a chart\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Automatic|0|Label is placed on the default position\. This is AutoPie for Pie charts, Right for Line Charts, Outside for BarCharts and Center for Stacked Bar charts\.<br />|
|Outside|1|Label is outside the data\. Applies to Bar, pie charts\.<br />|
|Inside|2|Label is inside the data\. Applies to Bar, pie charts\.<br />|
|Center|3|Label is centered on the data\. Applies to Bar, line, pie charts\.<br />|
|Axis|4|Label is placed on the Axis\. Applies to Bar charts\.<br />|
|Above|5|Label is placed Above the data\. Applies to Line charts\.<br />|
|Below|6|Label is placed Below the data\. Applies to Line charts\.<br />|
|Left|7|Label is placed Left of the data\. Applies to Line charts\.<br />|
|Right|8|Label is placed Right of the data\. Applies to Line charts\.<br />|
|AutoPie|9|Label is placed automatically on the chart\. This applies to Pie charts, and it is equivalent to TDataLabelPosition\.Automatic\.<br />|
|Any|10|Label has been manually moved and it is placed at an arbitrary place\.<br />|
