# TJpegOrientation Enumeration

Specifies the rotation of a JPEG image\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Normal|0|Image is not rotated\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 1, or no JPEG orientation present\.<br />|
|FlipHorizontal|1|Image is mirrored horizontally\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 2\.<br />|
|Rotate180|2|Image is rotated 180 degrees\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 3\.<br />|
|FlipVertical|3|Image is mirrored vertically\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 4\.<br />|
|FlipHorizontalAndRotate270CW|4|Image is rotated 270 degrees clockwise and then mirrored horizontally\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 5\.<br />|
|Rotate90CW|5|Image is rotated 90 degrees clockwise\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 6\.<br />|
|FlipHorizontalAndRotate90CW|6|Image is rotated 90 degrees clockwise and then mirrored horizontally\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 7\.<br />|
|Rotate270CW|7|Image is rotated 270 degrees clockwise\. Corresponds to JPEG orientation 6\.<br />|
