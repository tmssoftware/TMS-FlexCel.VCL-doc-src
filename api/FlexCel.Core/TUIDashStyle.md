# TUIDashStyle Enumeration

Platform independent line style\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Solid|0|Specifies a solid line\.|
|Dot|1|Specifies a line consisting of dots\.|
|SmallDash|2|Specifies a line consisting of small dashes, smaller than dashes but longer than dots\.|
|Dash|3|Specifies a line consisting of dashes\.|
|DashDot|4|Specifies a line consisting of a repeating pattern of dash\-dot\.|
|LongDash|5|Specifies a line consisting of big dashes\.|
|LongDashDot|6|Specifies a line consisting of a repeating pattern of large dashes and dots\.|
|LongDashDotDot|7|Specifies a line consisting of a repeating pattern of large dash\-dot\-dot\.|
