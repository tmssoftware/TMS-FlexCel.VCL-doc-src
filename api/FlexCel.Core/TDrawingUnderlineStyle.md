# TDrawingUnderlineStyle Enumeration

Possible underline types in a drawing\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|None|0|No underline\.<br />|
|Words|1|Underline just the words and not the spaces between them\.<br />|
|Single|2|Underline the text with a single line of normal thickness\.<br />|
|Double|3|Underline the text with two lines of normal thickness\.<br />|
|Heavy|4|Underline the text with a single, thick line\.<br />|
|Dotted|5|Underline the text with a single, dotted line of normal thickness\.<br />|
|DottedHeavy|6|Underline the text with a single, thick dotted line\.<br />|
|Dash|7|Underline the text with a single, dashed line of normal thickness\.<br />|
|DashHeavy|8|Underline the text with a single, thick dashed line\.<br />|
|DashLong|9|Underline the text with a single line of normal thickness consisting of long dashes\.<br />|
|DashLongHeavy|10|Underline the text with a single thick line consisting of long dashes\.<br />|
|DotDash|11|Underline the text with a single line consisting of alternating dots and dashes of normal thcikness\.<br />|
|DotDashHeavy|12|Underline the text with a single thick line consisting of alternating dots and dashes\.<br />|
|DotDotDash|13|Underline the text with a single line of normal thickness consisting of repeating two dots and dashes\.<br />|
|DotDotDashHeavy|14|Underline the text with a single thick line consisting of repeating two dots and dashes\.<br />|
|Wavy|15|Underline the text with a single wavy line of normal thickness\.<br />|
|WavyHeavy|16|Underline the text with a single, thick wavy line\.<br />|
|WavyDouble|17|Underline the text with two wavy lines of normal thickness\.<br />|
