# TChartRadarStyle Enumeration

Style for the radar chart\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Standard|0|Specifies that the radar chart shall have lines but no markers and no fill\.<br />|
|Marker|1|Specifies that the radar chart shall have lines and markers but no fill\.<br />|
|Filled|2|Specifies that the radar chart shall be filled and have lines but no markers\.<br />|
