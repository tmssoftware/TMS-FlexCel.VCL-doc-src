# THeaderAndFooterKind Enumeration

Different kinds of headers and footers depending on which pages they apply\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Core](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|Default|0|This applies to all headers and footers that don't have a dedicated definition\.<br />|
|FirstPage|1|This applies to the header and footer of the first pages, when it is defined different than the others\.<br />|
|EvenPages|2|This applies to the headers and footes of even pages, when they are different than odd pages\.<br />|
