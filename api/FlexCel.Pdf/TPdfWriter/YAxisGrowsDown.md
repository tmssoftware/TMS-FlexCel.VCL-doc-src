# TPdfWriter.YAxisGrowsDown Property

When true, the y axis origin corresponds to the upper corner of a sheet and bigger Y coordinates will  move down on the paper\. This is the standard GDI\+ behavior\.
When false, the Y origin is at the bottom and it grows up into the page\. This is the standard PDF behavior\.
