# TPageLayoutDisplay Enumeration

How the pages will display when the document is opened for the first time\.


## Syntax

**Unit:** [FlexCel.Pdf](index.md)

## Members

|Name|Value|Description|
|---|---|---|
|None|0|Don't specify any setting: Open the document as defined in the viewer\.<br />|
|SinglePage|1|Display one page at a time\.<br />|
|OneColumnContinuous|2|Display the pages in one continuous column\.<br />|
|TwoColumnContinuousLeft|3|Display  the  pages  in  two continuous columns,  with  odd\-numbered pages on the left\.<br />|
|TwoColumnContinuousRight|4|Display  the  pages  in  two continuous columns,  with  odd\-numbered pages on the right\.<br />|
|TwoPageLeft|5|Display the pages two at a time, with odd\-numbered pages on the left\.<br />|
|TwoPageRight|6|Display the pages two at a time, with odd\-numbered pages on the right\.<br />|
